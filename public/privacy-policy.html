Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026  ·  A product of Tactician Studios LLC, Kansas City, Missouri

Tactician Studios LLC ("Tactician Studios," "we," "us," or "our") operates Famotive, a family productivity and chore-management application that helps parents assign and manage household responsibilities and helps children complete them through a gamified experience (the "Service" or "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you, including special protections for children who use Famotive under a parent's or guardian's account.

By creating a Famotive account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1.Information We Collect

1.1 Information from Parents/Account Holders

When a parent or guardian ("Parent") creates a Famotive account, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Password (stored in encrypted/hashed form)
  • Household information you provide, such as family name and the number of child profiles created
  • Billing information, if you purchase a paid subscription or in-app feature (processed by our third-party payment processor; we do not store full payment card numbers)
  • Communications you send us, such as support requests

1.2 Information About Children

Child profiles are created and managed entirely by a Parent within the Parent's account. We intentionally limit what we collect directly from children:

  • A first name or nickname/username chosen by the Parent or child
  • An avatar or profile image selected from in-app options (we do not require or encourage uploading a real photo of a child)
  • An age range or birth year, if provided by the Parent, used only to tailor age-appropriate content
  • In-app activity: chores assigned and completed, XP earned, achievements unlocked, rewards redeemed, and progress toward family goals

We do not knowingly collect a child's email address, phone number, precise geolocation, or government-issued identifiers, and children cannot use Famotive without a Parent-created and Parent-managed account.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information)
  • Usage and log data (features used, session length, crash reports, app performance data)
  • General, non-precise location derived from IP address (e.g., city/region), used for purposes like timezone and language defaults
  • Push notification tokens, if you enable notifications
Children's Privacy & COPPA. Famotive is designed for use by families, including children under 13, but only through a Parent-controlled account. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child without prior verifiable parental consent obtained through the Parent's account setup, we do not use children's information for behavioral advertising or to build advertising profiles, and we do not condition a child's participation in the app on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary. Parents may review, edit, or request deletion of their child's information at any time by contacting us at privacy@famotive.org or through in-app account settings.

2.How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain Parent and child profiles and operate core features (assigning, tracking, and approving chores; awarding XP, achievements, and rewards; tracking family goals)
  • Personalize the experience, such as age-appropriate content and suggested chores
  • Send account-related communications, such as chore reminders, approval requests, and service updates
  • Process payments for optional subscriptions or premium features
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service, including fraud prevention and troubleshooting
  • Analyze aggregated, de-identified usage trends to improve Famotive's features and gamification design
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use children's personal information for targeted or behavioral advertising, and we do not sell children's personal information.

The Famotive app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software. We do not track you or your children across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID).

4.Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, including encryption in transit, access controls, and routine security review. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5.Data Retention

We retain Parent and child information for as long as the account remains active. If a Parent deletes the family account or a specific child profile, we will delete or de-identify the associated personal information within a commercially reasonable time, except where we are required to retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6.Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Parents may exercise these rights on behalf of their child's profile at any time. To make a request, contact us at privacy@famotive.org. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

You can also manage most information directly in the app: edit or delete a child profile, adjust notification preferences, or close your account from Account Settings.

7.International Users

Famotive is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may have data protection laws different from those of your country.

8.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify Parents by email or through an in-app notice before the changes take effect. The "Effective Date" above reflects the latest revision.
