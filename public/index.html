Questions

Beta FAQ

Does the beta cost anything?

No. The beta is completely free.

Why does Android need a Google Group?

Google Play only lets people on an approved tester list install apps that are still in testing. Our Google Group is that list — joining it is what unlocks the download. You won’t get spammed; it’s only used for tester access.

Google Play says I’m not eligible / the app isn’t found

This almost always means the Play Store is using a different Google account than the one that joined the group. Switch accounts in the Play Store app (tap your profile picture), or join the group with the account your phone uses. After joining, give it a few minutes before trying again.

The Google Group says I don’t have permission

Sign in to Google first (tap “Sign in” at the top of the page), then reload — the “Join group” button only appears once you’re signed in.

Do my kids need their own phones?

No. Kids can use their own device or a shared family tablet — each family member gets their own profile.

How do I leave the beta?

On iPhone, remove Famotive from TestFlight. On Android, open the “Become a tester” page again and choose “Leave the program”. You can also delete your data at any time.
